# Hiring your own team instead

_Last checked November 8, 2026._

Your own developers learn the business from the inside and stay as long as you employ them. That takes
recruiting, salaries and someone who can judge technical work, all before the first release pays any of it back.

## If the software is the company

Then hire. A product you'll change every week for years, with decisions that need someone in the room every
day, belongs with your own team. And if you already have a technical lead who can hire and review, building
in-house keeps the knowledge where you'll need it.

## Before you've hired anyone

| Step | Your own team | With Zaions |
|---|---|---|
| Starting | recruiting first | a free consultation, then a [package](../packages.mdx) |
| Ongoing work | salaries every month | the Team package, a monthly mix of development, design, video, QA and AI work |
| Judging progress | you need someone technical | a link to the working build at every [milestone](../how-a-project-runs.mdx) |
| Later | the knowledge stays with them | the [handover](../handover.mdx) moves everything into your name |

## Us first, your team later

The two routes aren't either-or. We can build the first version and hand it to the people you hire. The
handover is written for exactly that day: your new team starts from what we had, with the repository, the
design files and the accounts already in your name. The website's comparison is at
[zaions.com/compare/in-house](https://zaions.com/compare/in-house).
