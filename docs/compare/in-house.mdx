# Hiring your own team instead

_Last checked November 8, 2026._

Your own developers learn the business from the inside and stay as long as you employ them. That takes
recruiting, salaries and someone who can judge technical work before the first release pays any of it back.

## When is your own team the better choice?

When the software is the company: a product you'll change every week for years, with decisions that need
someone in the room every day. If you already have a technical lead who can hire and review, building in-house
keeps the knowledge where you'll need it.

## How does working with Zaions compare?

| Step | Your own team | With Zaions |
|---|---|---|
| Starting | recruiting first | a free consultation, then a [package](../packages.mdx) |
| Ongoing work | salaries every month | the Team package, a monthly mix of development, design, video, QA and AI work |
| Judging progress | you need someone technical | a link to the working build at every [milestone](../how-a-project-runs.mdx) |
| Later | the knowledge stays with them | the [handover](../handover.mdx) moves everything into your name |

## Building first, hiring later

You can do both: we build the first version, and your team takes it over. The handover is written for
that moment, so another team starts from exactly what we had. The side-by-side is on
[zaions.com/compare/in-house](https://zaions.com/compare/in-house).
