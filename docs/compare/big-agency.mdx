# Coming from a big agency

_Last checked November 8, 2026._

A big agency sells a large bench of people under one contract, usually with an account manager between you
and the people building. For some projects, that bench is exactly what you're paying for.

## What the bench is for

Many specialists working at the same hour. If you're building a large platform on a fixed launch date, running
a brand campaign beside the build, or need support with round-the-clock cover written into the contract, that's
what an agency's size is for. Go with them. The same goes if your procurement asks for a large vendor with a
long client list: that's a fair requirement, and a big agency meets it.

## One brief, followed through both

| Step | With a big agency | With Zaions |
|---|---|---|
| Your first question | goes to an account manager | goes to the people who'll build it |
| The plan | often after a discovery phase | a written plan and price against your [brief](../brief.mdx) |
| Progress | status reports and meetings | a link to the working build at every [milestone](../how-a-project-runs.mdx) |
| The end | set by the contract | the [handover](../handover.mdx): everything in your name, a standard term |

The last row is the one to read twice. With us, the repository, the design files and the accounts moving into
your name isn't something you negotiate; every package has it.
