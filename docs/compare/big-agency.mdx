# Coming from a big agency

_Last checked November 8, 2026._

A big agency sells a large bench of people under one contract, usually with an account manager between you
and the people building. Here's how the same project runs with us, step by step.

## When is a big agency the better choice?

When your project needs many specialists working at the same hour: a large platform on a fixed launch date, a
brand campaign beside the build, or support with round-the-clock cover written into the contract. It also suits
a buyer whose procurement asks for a large vendor with a long client list.

## How does the same project run with Zaions?

| Step | With a big agency | With Zaions |
|---|---|---|
| Your first question | goes to an account manager | goes to the people who'll build it |
| The plan | often after a discovery phase | a written plan and price against your [brief](../brief.mdx) |
| Progress | status reports and meetings | a link to the working build at every [milestone](../how-a-project-runs.mdx) |
| The end | set by the contract | the [handover](../handover.mdx): everything in your name, a standard term |

## What to bring if you already have an agency quote

Send us the same brief you sent them. Your estimate from us names the package that fits, and if a cheaper one
covers the job, it says so. See [Packages](../packages.mdx) for what each one covers, and the side-by-side on
[zaions.com/compare/big-agency](https://zaions.com/compare/big-agency).
