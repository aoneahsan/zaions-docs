# Other ways to get it built, compared

_Last checked November 8, 2026._

Hiring us is one way to get a product built. There are five others founders usually weigh, and each one is the
better choice for some jobs. These pages walk through how a Zaions project runs next to each of them, so you
can tell which one fits yours.

| If you're weighing | Read | It's the better choice when |
|---|---|---|
| A big agency | [A big agency](./big-agency.mdx) | the work needs a lot of people at the same hour |
| One freelancer | [A solo freelancer](./solo-freelancer.mdx) | it's one small job with a clear finish line |
| An AI app builder or no-code tool | [Building it yourself with AI](./ai-builder.mdx) | you want to test an idea before you spend on it |
| Your own developers | [Hiring your own team](./in-house.mdx) | the software is the business for years to come |
| Developers billed by the hour | [Developers by the hour](./hourly-developers.mdx) | you already have someone to direct them |

## What stays the same in every Zaions project?

Four steps, whatever the route you're comparing against:

1. **The brief.** You say what it must do, who uses it and your budget range.
2. **A written plan and price**, before anyone writes code.
3. **Milestones**, each ending with a link to the working build.
4. **The handover**, where the repository, the design files, the accounts and the passwords move into your name.

AI agents write most of our code now. A person on the team decides what they build, reads what comes back and
runs it before a milestone link reaches you. The same comparison, with dated examples from our own projects, is
on [zaions.com/compare](https://zaions.com/compare).
