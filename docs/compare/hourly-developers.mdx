# Coming from hourly developers

_Last checked November 8, 2026._

Some firms rent out developers by the hour or the month and leave the direction to you. With the right lead on
your side, that's flexible and quick to scale.

## When are hourly developers the better choice?

When you have a technical lead who writes the tasks and reviews the code, and you need more hands for a stretch
of work. It's also a good way to add one skill your team lacks for a few months.

## How does the same project run with Zaions?
