# Coming from hourly developers

_Last checked November 8, 2026._

Some firms rent out developers by the hour or the month and leave the direction to you. With the right lead on
your side, that's flexible and quick to scale.

## Do you have someone to direct them?

That's the whole question. If a technical lead on your side writes the tasks and reviews the code, and you need
more hands for a stretch of work, rent them. It's also a good way to add one skill your team lacks for a few
months without changing how your team runs.

## Who carries what

Without that lead, the plan, the priorities and the quality checks still land on you. Here's how the load
shifts:

| Step | Developers by the hour | With Zaions |
|---|---|---|
| Who plans the work | you | we do, in a written plan against your [brief](../brief.mdx) |
| What sets the price | the hours | the written estimate |
| A change in scope | more hours | estimated in writing first; the price moves only when you agree |
| Progress | tasks closed on your board | a link to the working build at every [milestone](../how-a-project-runs.mdx) |

## Halfway through with hourly developers?

Send the brief and, if work has started, the repository. We estimate what's left from where the code stands,
and the project ends with the [handover](../handover.mdx). Both routes are compared on the website at
[zaions.com/compare/hourly-developers](https://zaions.com/compare/hourly-developers).
