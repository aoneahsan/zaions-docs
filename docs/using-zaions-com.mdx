# Getting around zaions.com

zaions.com has two layouts. A phone gets one built for a thumb; a desktop browser keeps the website you'd
expect. This page covers both, plus the settings and notices you'll meet on the way.

## What does zaions.com look like on a phone?

Open it in a phone browser and it lays itself out like an app. That happens when the screen is narrow and
you're using touch, so a narrowed desktop window still gets the web layout.

| Where | What's there |
|---|---|
| Top bar | Zaions, **Take a tour of this page**, Appearance and **Menu** |
| Bottom tabs | Services · Work · Packages · Team · FAQ |
| Above the tabs | **Start a project**, within reach of your thumb. It steps aside while the page's own Start a project button is on screen. |
| Card rows | Swipe sideways, or tap a dot under the row to jump to a card |
| Footer | Folded into one button. Tap it for every footer link. |

**Menu** opens a sheet with the main pages, **Start a project** and **Book a free consultation**. Below them
sit two groups: **Company** (About, Compare, Blog, Contact and more) and **Legal** (the policies, the
[sitemap](https://zaions.com/sitemap) and the [feed](https://zaions.com/feed)).

Turn the phone sideways and the layout follows. No reload.

## What can I change in the Appearance panel?

Press **Appearance** at the top of any page.

| Setting | Choices |
|---|---|
| Mode | Dark · Light · System |
| Color | Orchid · Ion · Forge |
| Corners | Square · Small · Medium · Large · Round |
| Spacing | Compact · Comfortable · Spacious |
| Text size | Small · Medium · Large |
| Typeface | Studio · Readable |
| Motion | System · Full · Reduced |

Choices are saved in this browser, not on our servers. **Reset to defaults** puts everything back. If your
browser blocks storage, your choices last until you close the page.

## What is the bar at the bottom on my first visit?

It's the notice bar. It says: *"By using Zaions, you agree to our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. We
count how zaions.com gets used, so we know what to fix next."*

Press **Okay** or Escape to close it. Otherwise it leaves after 10 seconds; hover over it or focus it and it
waits. It shows once in each browser.

The counting is three analytics tools, and they're always on. There's no switch for them. The
[Privacy Policy](https://zaions.com/privacy) says what each one receives, and the
[Cookie Policy](https://zaions.com/cookie-policy) lists the cookies they set. Block cookies in your browser
if you'd rather; the site still works.

## What is "Our own products" above the footer?

Four cards for other products the Zaions team makes and runs. We choose them. No ad network is involved, and
nothing about you is sent to those products.

A card opens that product's own site in a new tab, under its own policy. **See every product** goes to
[Products](https://zaions.com/products). The cards don't appear on Start a project, where you're writing a
brief, or on Products, which already lists them all.
