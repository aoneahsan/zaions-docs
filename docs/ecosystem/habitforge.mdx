# HabitForge

![](/img/ecosystem/habitforge.svg)

Every check-in changes the rope that shows your consistency.

## A check-in made visible as a rope

Turns consistency into a rope you can see. A rope that thickens with every check-in and frays where you stopped.

## What else does it offer?

Friends, challenges and a leaderboard you can leave. Web and Android, from one account.
