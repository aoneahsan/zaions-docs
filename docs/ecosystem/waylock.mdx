# Waylock

![](/img/ecosystem/waylock.svg)

Hold chosen stops in place while the rest of a route changes.

## Chosen stops that stay in a changing route

Waylock is an Android planning layer over Google Maps for drivers and couriers. A driver can hold chosen stops in place and order the rest around them.

## What else does it offer?

After a wrong turn, the chosen route stays visible beside the option to return or take a new route.
