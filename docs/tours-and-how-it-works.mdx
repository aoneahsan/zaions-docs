# Page tours and how working with Zaions works

zaions.com shows you around in two ways: a short tour of the page you're on, and one page that walks
through how working with Zaions works, from the first look to handover.

## How do I take a tour of a page?

Press **Take a tour of this page**, the button beside the appearance control at the top of the page. The
tour points at each part of the page in turn.

The tour runs on five pages: the [home page](https://zaions.com/), [Services](https://zaions.com/services),
[Our work](https://zaions.com/portfolio), [Pricing](https://zaions.com/pricing) and
[Start a project](https://zaions.com/start-a-project).

The first time you open one of these pages, a small note says *"New here? Take a quick tour of this page."*
Choose **Show me** to start, or **Dismiss**. The note shows once per page on your device.

## What can I do during a tour?

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Step 2 of 6 | Where you are in the tour |
| All steps | The list of steps; pick one to jump to it |
| Back · Next · Done | Move through the steps |
| Skip tour | Stop the tour at any step |
| Close the tour | Close it; Escape does the same |

On a phone the step card sits at the bottom of the screen, so the part it points at stays in view.

## What does each tour show?

| Page | Steps |
|---|---|
| Home | Who we are · What we do · How we work · What you own · Proof · Packages |
| Services | Services · Pick a service · How every service runs · Proof you can open |
| Our work | Our work · Every product, live · Earlier projects · Case studies |
| Pricing | Three packages · What every package includes · Add-ons and separate estimates · Find your package |
| Start a project | Send us the brief · Three short steps · Tell us the idea |

## What is "How working with Zaions works"?

[How working with Zaions works](https://zaions.com/how-zaions-works) is six steps, from the first look to
handover. Each one shows the page where it happens.

1. **See what we build.** Eight services, one team.
2. **Check our work.** Products we built and still run. Open any of them and judge.
3. **Pick a package.** Three packages, and the first call is free. Find yours in two questions.
4. **Send the brief.** Three steps, two required fields. If your idea is still a napkin sketch, that's a
   fine place to start.
5. **Say it instead of typing.** Tell us the idea the way you'd tell a friend, typed or by voice. We draft
   the fields from it; you change anything before you send.
6. **Plan, milestones, handover.** You'll see the plan, the price and the first milestone before we write a
   line of code. Each milestone ends with a link to the build. Everything you paid for moves into your name.

To open a step directly, add `?step=` and its number, for example
[zaions.com/how-zaions-works?step=4](https://zaions.com/how-zaions-works?step=4). The arrow keys move between
steps, and so does a swipe on a phone.
