# What you'll see, and what you'll own

Hiring a team to build software usually means trusting a process you can't see. These pages show every step
of how we work, and list what moves into your name at the end, so you know both before you send a word.

Zaions is a software agency. We build web and Android apps, browser extensions and desktop apps, and we do
the design, video, QA and AI work around them. We also built and still run <ProductCount /> products of our own.

Four steps, every project. You send the brief. You get a written plan and estimate before we write a line of
code. Every milestone ends with a link to the working build, so you click through progress instead of
reading a status report, and at handover the code, the design files, the accounts and every credential
move into your name.

## Who are these docs for?

You, if you're thinking about hiring us, or you already have.

They're written for founders and small-business owners who know what the business needs and don't want to
babysit developers to get it. We assume no technical background. Where a technical word is unavoidable, it
comes with its meaning in the same sentence, like a code repository, where all your source code lives.

## How do these docs relate to zaions.com?

zaions.com is the short version. These pages are the long one.

The agency site tells you what we do and shows the products we run. The docs explain how each part of a
project works: what a good brief contains, what a milestone link opens, what moves into your name at
handover, and how to pay an invoice once you're a client.

These pages quote a few figures, like the package prices and the phone number. zaions.com is the authority
for them, and where the two differ, zaions.com wins:

- the current package prices, on [Pricing](https://zaions.com/pricing)
- the payment methods, which only zaions.com lists, on [Payments](https://zaions.com/payments)
- the phone number and email addresses, on [Contact](https://zaions.com/contact)

If a figure here differs from your written estimate, the figures in your estimate are the ones for your
project. These pages are written for everyone.
