Getting around zaions.com

zaions.com has two layouts. A phone gets one built for a thumb; a desktop browser keeps the website you'd expect. This page covers both, plus the settings and notices you'll meet on the way.

What does zaions.com look like on a phone?​

Open it in a phone browser and it lays itself out like an app. That happens when the screen is narrow and you're using touch, so a narrowed desktop window still gets the web layout.

WhereWhat's there
Top barZaions, Take a tour of this page, Appearance and Menu
Bottom tabsServices · Work · Packages · Team · FAQ
Above the tabsStart a project, within reach of your thumb. It steps aside while the page's own Start a project button is on screen.
Card rowsSwipe sideways, or tap a dot under the row to jump to a card
FooterFolded into one button. Tap it for every footer link.

Menu opens a sheet with the main pages, Start a project and Book a free consultation. Below them sit two groups: Company (About, Compare, Blog, Contact and more) and Legal (the policies, the sitemap (opens in a new tab) and the feed (opens in a new tab)).

Turn the phone sideways and the layout follows. No reload.

What can I change in the Appearance panel?​

Press Appearance at the top of any page.

SettingChoices
ModeDark · Light · System
ColorOrchid · Ion · Forge
CornersSquare · Small · Medium · Large · Round
SpacingCompact · Comfortable · Spacious
Text sizeSmall · Medium · Large
TypefaceStudio · Readable
MotionSystem · Full · Reduced

Choices are saved in this browser, not on our servers. Reset to defaults puts everything back. If your browser blocks storage, your choices last until you close the page.

What is the bar at the bottom on my first visit?​

It's the notice bar. It says: "By using Zaions, you agree to our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. We count how zaions.com gets used, so we know what to fix next."

Press Okay or Escape to close it. Otherwise it leaves after 10 seconds; hover over it or focus it and it waits. It shows once in each browser.

The counting is three analytics tools, and they're always on. There's no switch for them. The Privacy Policy (opens in a new tab) says what each one receives, and the Cookie Policy (opens in a new tab) lists the cookies they set. Block cookies in your browser if you'd rather; the site still works.

What is "Our own products" above the footer?​

Four cards for other products the Zaions team makes and runs. We choose them. No ad network is involved, and nothing about you is sent to those products.

A card opens that product's own site in a new tab, under its own policy. See every product goes to Products (opens in a new tab). The cards don't appear on Start a project, where you're writing a brief, or on Products, which already lists them all.
